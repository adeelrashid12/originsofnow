--- CREATIVE BRIEF ---
Creative Brief —Website Page
Brand Overview
Origins of Now is a movement for intelligent living — a brand built on truth, clarity, and elevated standards. It offers products, tools, and insights that help people live better, think clearer, and reconnect with what truly matters. Every touchpoint with the brand should make people feel grounded, informed, and inspired to “Live by it.”
Objective
To design a dynamic and intuitive website that becomes the central hub for customers and prospects — offering access to guidance, research, product updates, and a referral system that grows the Origins of Now community organically.
Core Functions
 1. Customer Access Portal:
 • Personalized login for product owners.
 • Product-specific guides, tutorials, FAQs, and ongoing updates.
 2. Referral & Recommendation Form:
 • Seamless, built-in feature for customers to recommend friends or family.
 • Simple form capturing name and phone number.
 • Optional message or “why I recommend this” field.
 3. Prospect Sign-Up Section:
 • For those not yet ready to purchase.
 • Subscription to newsletters, exclusive previews, and health insights.
 • Offers and event notifications to keep them engaged until conversion.
 4. Knowledge & Community Hub:
 • Brief, categorized articles and videos about holistic wellness, detox, EMF protection, hydration, and lifestyle upgrades.
 • Linked to verified research or in-house studies for credibility.
 5. Product & Support Integration:
 • Dynamic product library with clean navigation and interactive visuals.
 • Support center for warranty, maintenance, and customer care.
Target Audience
Primary:
Conscious individuals who prioritize quality, health, and authenticity — those who seek clarity amid the overwhelming noise of conflicting wellness advice. They are ready for truth-based, intelligent solutions that simplify and elevate their daily living.
Secondary:
Health enthusiasts, professionals, and community influencers who value consistency, integrity, and science-backed guidance — and want to share reliable, high-standard products with others.
Emotional Landscape
 • Experiencing information fatigue and confusion due to the overload of wellness advice and trends.
 • Desire clarity, simplicity, and stability that they can trust and integrate.
 • Seek peace of mind and alignment as much as physical well-being.
Behavioral Traits
 • Research-oriented yet selective — they verify before they buy.
 • Motivated by authentic recommendations, not aggressive selling.
 • Engage in trust-based word-of-mouth sharing.
 • Appreciate efficiency, organization, and intuitive digital experiences.
Aspirations & Values
 • Seek products aligned with holistic wellness, integrating body, mind, and environment.
 • Gravitate toward brands that feel exclusive yet inclusive — refined, not elitist.
 • Want to belong to a movement that stands for truth, clarity, and elevated living.
Design Implications
 • The website must feel calm, intelligent, and trustworthy.
 • Prioritize clean, minimal layouts using nature-inspired tones (sky/water blues, deep purples, earth hues).
 • Deliver clarity in structure — intuitive menus, quick links, and visible calls to action.
 • Incorporate visual metaphors of stillness, flow, and light — a visual antidote to chaos.
 • Copy tone: Grounded, clarifying, and empowering
Products — Website Direction
Overall Objective
The website should initially focus on two core products. These should be positioned as premium, high-quality solutions designed to support a healthier, smarter, and more considered way of living at home.
The purpose of the product section is not simply to sell the products. It is to educate, build trust, create desire, and allow the visitor to arrive at the product as a natural solution to the needs and questions they have explored throughout the website.
Positioning
The products should be presented as:
Premium, high-end appliances that belong in a modern, health-conscious home.
Products associated with quality, thoughtful living, and prioritising health and wellbeing.
Solutions that are relevant to people who take a proactive approach to their health and lifestyle.
Products worth understanding before making a purchasing decision.
The overall impression should be aspirational and credible rather than commercial. The visitor should feel that these are considered products for people who value quality and wellbeing; not products being aggressively sold to them.
Tone & User Experience
The presentation should feel:
Premium · Intelligent · Credible · Aspirational · Educational · Trustworthy
Avoid a traditional sales approach based heavily on discounts, urgency, exaggerated claims, or hard-sell language.
Instead, the page should create curiosity and encourage the visitor to learn, explore the evidence, understand the technology, and discover how the products may fit into their lifestyle.
The Desired Visitor Journey
The website should guide the visitor through a natural progression:
Understand the need → Explore the information → Learn about the solution → Understand the product → Explore the evidence → Feel confident about the choice
By the time the visitor reaches the product, it should feel like a logical and compelling solution to what they have just learned, rather than an advertisement placed in front of them.
Key Message
The products should ultimately be presented as a meaningful investment in the quality of everyday life, products that can become an important part of a home where health, wellbeing, and quality are treated as priorities.
The goal is to leave the visitor thinking:
“This is something I should understand better.”
rather than:
“They are trying to sell me something.”
